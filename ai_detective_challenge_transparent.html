AI says...
“A rectangle with a length of 8 cm and width of 5 cm has an area of 40 cm².”

Your detective mission 🔎

Case solved! AI is correct. ✅
A = length × width
A = 8 × 5 = 40 cm²
The area of a rectangle is found by multiplying its length by its width. Since 8 × 5 = 40, AI's answer is correct. Remember to include the square unit, cm², because area measures a surface.
🌟 Detective reminder: Do not believe AI immediately. Verify its answer using a mathematical formula, your own calculation and clear reasoning.
